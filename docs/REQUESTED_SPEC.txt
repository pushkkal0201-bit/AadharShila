# 2. Product purpose

Aadharshila evaluates a mortgage application by connecting:

**Applicant information → authorized evidence → verification → affordability → credit risk → property assessment → policies and compliance controls → decision → explanation → audit and reassessment.**

Every assessment must answer:

1. Which information was submitted?
2. Which information came from an external source?
3. Which facts are verified?
4. Which facts are missing, stale, contradictory, or uncertain?
5. What income and obligations were accepted, and why?
6. What loan terms satisfy the configured affordability and collateral rules?
7. Which policies and controls applied?
8. What risk estimate was produced, and what are its limitations?
9. Why was the application approved, rejected, or referred?
10. What is the next permitted action?
11. Can the original decision be reproduced later?

The engine must return:

- **APPROVE**
- **REJECT**
- **REFER**

A referral is a completed routing decision, not completed credit underwriting.

Straight-through approval is permitted only when all mandatory prerequisites are satisfied.
