# Demonstration data manifest

- **Provenance:** Synthetic records generated by `src/aadharshila/seed.py`.
- **Licensing:** Authored as part of this codebase; no external personal data or licensed bureau dataset supplied.
- **Generation:** 16 fixed case definitions and deterministic, explicitly simulated evidence values.
- **Intended use:** Test whether the same calculation, evidence, policy and decision functions handle different underwriting conditions.
- **Limitations:** Not statistically representative of Indian borrowers or property markets; no outcome labels for delinquency/default, no model-training provenance or probability-of-default validation.
